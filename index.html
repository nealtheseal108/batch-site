Who we are

UNC's second men's team. Built to compete.

BatCH is UNC Chapel Hill's second men's ultimate team and a recognized UNC student organization. It plays a full fall and spring tournament schedule across the Carolinas and the Southeast and competes every spring in the college series run by USA Ultimate, the sport's national governing body.

What BatCH plays for

  1. Compete. Every spring, BatCH plays the USA Ultimate college series, from the Southern Atlantic Coast conference championships to Atlantic Coast Regionals. In spring 2026, it made Atlantic Coast D-I Regionals for the first time since 2019.
  2. Hold a standard. BatCH practices three nights a week under the lights at Hooker Fields and tracks stats for every point of its fall tournament games.
  3. Play for each other. BatCH wins as a team. Leaders come from across the roster, not just the captains, and each class passes that standard on to the next.

History

Eleven seasons on USA Ultimate.

USA Ultimate's online records for BatCH start in 2014–15, when the team was listed as B-atCH. It has been BatCH ever since. Each season shows the USA Ultimate events the team entered.

    CC = conference championship. Dev = USA Ultimate's college Developmental division. D-I = Division I. Source: USA Ultimate team pages, 2014–15 to 2025–26. Seasons before 2014–15 are not on USA Ultimate's site with rosters.

      The daily game

      Batchillion

      Batchillion is BatCH's own daily game. Each prompt asks you to name a teammate, and every correct name is a throw down the field. The rarer the pick, the farther it flies: a name almost nobody else thinks of is a one in a batchillion and goes 100 yards, all the way into the end zone.

      • Players
        Seven prompts a day, the same for everyone, 40 seconds each: name a teammate who started university in a given year. Once you use someone in Players, they're gone for you for good.
      • Stat sheets
        Rounds built from this season's game stats, like who threw an assist at Bank Run or who got a block at Bare Bones.
      • Leaderboard
        Public, and it ranks your best single round.
